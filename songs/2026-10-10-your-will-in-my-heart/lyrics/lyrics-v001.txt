[Intro]
[Soft fingerpicked acoustic guitar]

[Verse 1]
Evening settles on the fields,
Daylight leaves the windowpane.
I lay my plans before You, King,
And softly speak Your name.
Jesus, all my days are Yours,
Every road and everything.
Here beside this quiet light,
I give my heart to You, my King.

[Chorus]
Jesus, my King, Your will be done,
In every word, in all my ways.
All power is Yours, Your throne stands sure,
Through changing winds and passing days.
Holy Spirit, draw me close,
Help me hear and follow through.
Let my love say yes today,
And let my footsteps honor You.

[Verse 2]
When the morning calls me out
To the work my hands must do,
Let the kindness in my voice
Show that I belong to You.
When You ask me to forgive,
When You lead me to make peace,
Let me gladly do Your will,
Even when it costs me ease.

[Instrumental]
[Quiet acoustic guitar and soft pedal steel]

[Verse 3]
Holy Spirit, here with me,
You know all I hold inside.
When my heart would turn away,
Gently bring it to the light.
Teach me how to walk with You,
Keep me mindful through the day.
When I hear the King’s command,
Help me answer and obey.

[Bridge]
Over every land and sea,
Over all that breathes and lives,
Jesus, Yours the right to rule,
Yours the word that stands and lives.
Let Your Kingdom come through me,
In the small things love can do.
With a willing, quiet heart,
I will give my yes to You.

[Outro]
Holy Spirit, here with me,
As the night grows still and deep.
Jesus, You are King forever,
All my days are Yours to lead.
