# RUACH Anthem — approved identity

Concept 02, **Breath into Song**, approved by the user on 2026-10-10 with “Looks good. Please finalize.”

## Meaning and distinction

The flowing musical-note emblem expresses breath becoming melody: RUACH as spirit and breath, Anthem as a shared song of allegiance to Jesus the King. This is the music channel's distinct symbol. The parent RUACH two-ribbon mark remains a separate identity and must not be substituted for the Anthem emblem.

## Usage

- Brand text: **RUACH ANTHEM**. Preserve the spaced RUACH line and prominent serif ANTHEM treatment.
- Tagline: **Governed by the Spirit.** Omit at small sizes where it becomes unreadable.
- Palette targets: Crimson **#B21F32**, Obsidian **#141821**, Ivory **#F7F3ED**; white for the reversed symbol.
- Use the standalone emblem for avatars. Maintain generous clear space, at least one fifth of symbol width. Never stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows.
- Use quiet backgrounds and preserve strong contrast. Do not add institutional religious imagery.
- Earlier parent-logo explorations are superseded for the channel identity, but remain archived. Existing published videos are historical releases and are not retroactively changed.
